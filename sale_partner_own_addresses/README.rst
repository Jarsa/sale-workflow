This module keeps the addresses of a sales order within the customer:

- The customer is a top-level contact (a company or a person without
  parent).
- The invoice address is the customer itself by default; it can only be
  the customer or one of its invoice addresses.
- The delivery address is one of the delivery addresses of the customer,
  or the customer itself when it has none.

The rules apply in the sales order form (domains) and on save
(constraints).

Usage
=====

Create a quotation and pick a customer: only contacts without parent are
offered. The invoice address is set to the customer and the delivery
address to its first delivery address (or the customer). Both fields
only offer the addresses of that customer.
